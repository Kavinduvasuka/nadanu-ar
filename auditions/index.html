THIS IS NADANU 3.0

Presented by the Computing Society of ICBT, Nadanu 3.0 brings together passionate singers and dancers for an exciting performing arts competition. The auditions are designed to discover standout talent, creativity, teamwork, and stage presence.

Contestants showcase their abilities in four categories. The strongest performances are selected to advance to the Grand Finals — where the spotlight shines even brighter.
